Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Best Meal You Ever Had](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one sentence, at most
200 characters, about the best meal I ever had, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The best meal I ever had was a Rp10,000 plate of nasi uduk eaten on a curb at 6 a.m., coconut rice warm in banana leaf, with a boiled egg my mother had peeled for me.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
